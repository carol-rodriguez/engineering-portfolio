## Overview

This project focused on the design of a mechanical testing mechanism to evaluate product durability through controlled drop-impact experiments.

## Engineering Approach

The project involved analyzing design requirements, developing mechanical concepts, creating CAD models, and evaluating the feasibility of the proposed mechanism.

The final design prioritized repeatability, manufacturability, and ease of testing.
