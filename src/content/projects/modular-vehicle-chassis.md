## Overview

This project involved the conceptual design of a modular vehicle chassis considering structural requirements, manufacturing constraints, and weight optimization.

## Engineering Approach

The design process included requirement analysis, concept generation, CAD development, and evaluation of different structural configurations.

Manufacturability and assembly considerations were integrated throughout the design process.
